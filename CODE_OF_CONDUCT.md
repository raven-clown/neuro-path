# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind, be patient, and assume good intent. Disagree about code, not about people.

## What we expect

- Welcome newcomers and questions of every level.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and that a no is about the project, not about you.
- Credit other people's work.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Publishing someone's private information
- Repeated off-topic posts or spam
- Any behavior that would be unwelcome in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space connected to this project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/raven-clown). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, lock threads, or block accounts that break these rules.
